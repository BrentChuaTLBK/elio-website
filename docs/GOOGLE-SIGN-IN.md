# Connect Google sign-in to Elio

The website already has customer and team Google sign-in buttons. They remain disabled while the Google provider is off in Elio's Supabase project, and activate automatically after it is enabled. Use Elio-specific credentials; do not copy the TLB Kitchen OAuth client or secrets.

1. In [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/), create or select a project for Elio. Open **Google Auth Platform → Branding** and enter the Elio app name, support email, and contact email. In **Audience**, choose **External** so customers can sign in. Add your own Google account as a test user while testing; publish the OAuth app for all customers when its required public information is ready.
2. Under **Clients**, create an OAuth client of type **Web application**. Add `https://eliocheesecakes.com` as an **Authorized JavaScript origin**. Add `https://dzxyhckkkrzqpwpavngn.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback` as an **Authorized redirect URI**. The Google redirect goes to Supabase, which then returns the visitor to the customer or team page.
3. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret** directly into Elio's [Supabase Auth provider settings](https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/dzxyhckkkrzqpwpavngn/auth/providers) under **Google**, enable the provider, and save. Do not send the secret in chat or commit it to GitHub.
4. In [Supabase Auth URL Configuration](https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/dzxyhckkkrzqpwpavngn/auth/url-configuration), keep the Site URL `https://eliocheesecakes.com`. Add exact Redirect URLs `https://eliocheesecakes.com/account.html` and `https://eliocheesecakes.com/admin-account.html`. For local testing, also add `http://127.0.0.1:4173/account.html` and `http://127.0.0.1:4173/admin-account.html`. Hosted settings must be saved in the dashboard; the repo's `supabase/config.toml` is only the local configuration.
5. Test a customer sign-in at `https://eliocheesecakes.com/account.html` and the owner's team sign-in at `https://eliocheesecakes.com/admin-account.html`. The customer should return to the customer page. The owner should be able to open `/manage.html` after returning to the team page. A customer must not gain dashboard access. Also test sign-out, email registration, verification, and password reset.

Before publishing an External Google OAuth app for unrestricted customer use, provide a public privacy policy and terms links on Elio's homepage and in Google Auth Platform branding. Google's [OAuth policy](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/policies) requires them; the final policy content must reflect Elio's actual data practices and should be supplied by the business. While the app is in **Testing**, only added test users can complete sign-in.

References: [Supabase Google sign-in](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/social-login/auth-google), [Supabase redirect URLs](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/redirect-urls), [Google OAuth policies](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/policies).
