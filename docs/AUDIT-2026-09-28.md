## Findings requiring attention

1. **The storefront currently has no available ordering dates.** October's menu is published with zero flavors selected. Configured future stock exists only through September 30, while normal boxes need three production days. In the admin, select the intended October flavors and enter the actual daily quantities; keep intentional blocked dates. No quantities were guessed during the audit.
2. **A product named “Test” is visible in the public catalog.** It was already present before this audit. Review whether it should be hidden or deleted through the protected catalog controls. It was not silently removed.
3. **No DMARC DNS record is configured.** Resend confirms SPF and DKIM are verified. A Cloudflare write to add a non-enforcing policy failed with `Authentication error`; no DNS change was made. Add a TXT record named `_dmarc` with value `v=DMARC1; p=none;` and TTL 300 (or Auto). This is a starting policy, not enforcement or report collection. [Cloudflare authentication documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/concepts/email-authentication/) explains the policy choices.
4. **Supabase reports leaked-password protection disabled.** This existing Auth warning remains. No subscription or plan changes were made to enable a feature that may depend on the project plan.

## Production checks

Controlled tests invoked the deployed database functions inside one transaction, then rolled back all fixtures:

- Website order: ₱1,000 subtotal, 5% promo, ₱50 discount, ₱950 total; three flavor pieces reserved; repeated submission returned the same order.
- Receipt metadata commit and approval: commission estimated at ₱95, payable only after completion; paid pickup eligible for calendar; Accounting net reconciled to ₱950.
- Affiliate: ₱50 payout recorded once despite a repeated request, added one expense, voided successfully, and refund reversed the earned commission and excluded the order from Accounting.
- Direct delivery: custom product used no stock, cash/change matched, pending delivery did not count as income, and collecting the later ₱300 fee increased recorded income correctly. The order remained calendar eligible.
- Event: a ₱130 sale from 100 separately prepared pouches left 99, calculated ₱70 change from ₱200, completed immediately, posted income and stayed out of the calendar.

After rollback, the four pre-existing orders remained, no audit product fixtures remained, and the order email queue had no pending messages. Existing order arithmetic reconciled to ₱4,760 of current Accounting sales, with no negative website/event stock or orphaned allocations.

Both email and calendar cron jobs are active every minute. Recent HTTP responses were all 200 (120 in the initial one-hour window; 20 in the final ten-minute check). Newsletter queued/contact-sync errors were absent. Receipt buckets are private; the product-image bucket is public as intended. The only stored image at the inspection time was WebP.
